StagePatch – installable app package

1) Put this whole folder on any free HTTPS web host (GitHub Pages, Netlify Drop, Cloudflare Pages).
2) Open the site in Chrome on your Android phone, menu (three dots) > "Install app". It then opens full screen like a normal app, with MIDI working.
3) For a real .apk file: go to pwabuilder.com, enter your site address, choose Android, and download the package. Install the .apk on your phone (allow "install unknown apps").
   Keep the signing key PWABuilder gives you; you need it for updates.
